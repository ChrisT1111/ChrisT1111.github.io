Driftspark Labs / Arrowwords

The arrowword from the puzzle magazine, on your phone

The clue sits in a cell of the grid and an arrow shows where the answer goes. The puzzles are written in the language itself, not translated, and every one of them is inside the app. The first puzzles are in Dutch; more languages will follow.

Easy to play

  • Easy to read

    The clue for the selected word is shown large above the keyboard, so you never have to read the small cells. Zoom the grid if that reads better.

  • Three levels

    Easy, medium and hard. Harder puzzles have a bigger grid.

  • A built-in keyboard

    No autocorrect getting in the way. Tap a cell twice to switch direction.

  • Help when you are stuck

    Reveal a letter or a word, or check what you have filled in. That costs hints. You get free hints every day, and a short video earns you more.

  • No ads while you solve

    There are never ads while you are solving a puzzle. Buy Unlimited once for no ads at all and unlimited hints. No subscription.

  • Play anywhere

    All puzzles are inside the app. No internet and no account needed.
